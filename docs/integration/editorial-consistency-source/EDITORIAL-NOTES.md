# Editorial decisions and outstanding review items

## Digestive as the model, not a word quota

The Digestive sections have distinct jobs: General Overview builds the foundation quickly; Deep Dive goes into mechanisms and naming; How It Connects reads like an actual story; Who Cares? ends with an interesting implication; Exam Cram supports rapid scanning. Respiratory and Lymphoid retain subject-appropriate depth; their Deep Dives should not be made artificially short.

## What was changed

- Rewrote the Respiratory and Lymphoid General Overviews to bring them closer to Digestive's compact structure. Direct, bolded terms, clear routes, short explanations, no editorial narration about the course.
- Preserved 23 Respiratory and 19 Lymphoid Deep Dive sections and all source-slide metadata. Removed lecture/slide/professor self-references from student prose while retaining scientifically necessary qualifications, distinctions and examples. Broke some dense paragraphs into smaller units.
- Preserved the central narratives and all sections in both How It Connects articles. Retained only select bolded emphases instead of bolding nearly every term.
- Preserved all three Who Cares? stories in each system. Removed source-attribution commentary from the story endings in favor of the scientific consequence of each mechanism.
- Preserved original Detailed Reviews byte-for-byte at the parsed JSON content level. Added the two previously authored 18-section Quick Scan articles.
- Edited wrong answer options on 53 selected multiple-choice questions and made six source-aware flashcards more direct. No correct answers were changed.

## Material that still deserves human attention

1. The original instructor-aligned Detailed Reviews intentionally retain their slide/course terminology and occasional explanatory caveats. That is a deliberate mode distinction from Quick Scan, not a defect in the revised General Overview or Deep Dive.
2. The Lymphoid source material contains an ambiguous question about a single hormone supposedly required for B-cell differentiation. This is not converted into an invented fact. The Deep Dive now teaches B-cell development in bone marrow versus T-cell maturation in thymus directly; the editorial ambiguity remains documented in the original curriculum blueprint.
3. The revised prose should be visually checked on the actual site, particularly Notes anchors after changes to paragraph lengths. Stable section IDs alone cannot guarantee that an old text selection still reattaches.
4. The multiple-choice edits focus on conspicuously implausible distractors. They improve question quality but do not constitute independent scientific verification of each option of every question or all 1,433 Study Tools records. Further instructor feedback may justify selective edits.
5. Gallery licensing, attribution, image quality, 3D mesh mapping and web deployment have NOT been modified by this package.
