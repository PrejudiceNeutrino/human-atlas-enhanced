## Important qualifications for final scientific review

1. **Carbon dioxide transport.** The 70/23/7 distribution is an approximate teaching model. Most carbon dioxide-derived carbon in the 70% pathway travels as **bicarbonate (HCO₃⁻)** rather than free carbonic acid.
2. **Blood color.** Oxygenated and deoxygenated blood are shades of red, not literally red versus blue. The blue color on the slides is a diagram convention.
3. **Breathing centers.** 'Apneustic' and 'pneumotaxic' are historical functional labels. Modern brainstem control is distributed and interactive; DRG/VRG is a useful but simplified description.
4. **Airway histology.** Epithelial types are characteristic patterns, not sharp uniform boundaries across every microscopic airway subregion.
5. **Nasal nitric oxide.** The lecture mentions NO and oxygen uptake. This bank teaches the well-established filtering/warming/humidification functions without claiming that all proposed nasal-NO effects have been demonstrated to produce a fixed increase in oxygen uptake.
6. **Pulmonary physiology notation.** P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ is Boyle's law for a fixed amount of gas at constant temperature. Inspired air flows through an open system, but the inverse pressure–volume principle helps explain alveolar pressure changes during thoracic movement.
7. **Quiet versus forced breathing.** Quiet exhalation is primarily passive recoil; muscle recruitment and precise mechanical roles change with exertion and phase.
8. **Additional concepts.** Entries tagged `scopeNote` (e.g., pre-Bötzinger complex, club cells, FRC, respiratory acidosis) are explanatory scientific extensions, not necessarily named in the instructor slides. They are not needed to claim study-guide coverage.
9. **Answer validation.** Fill-in accepted alternatives are deliberately conservative. For example, 'hemoglobin' does not count as 'oxyhemoglobin,' and 'bronchi' does not count as 'bronchioles.'
10. **Question options.** Correct choice ID is `c1` for authoring convenience. The student-facing application must shuffle choice positions during each quiz session, keeping IDs stable.
