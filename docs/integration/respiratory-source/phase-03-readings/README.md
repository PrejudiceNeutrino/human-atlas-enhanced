## Source and accuracy policy

Course content derives from `23-Respiratory System.pptx`, `Respiratory - Copy.pptx`, and `23-Study Guide.docx`. The source slides are not bundled. Slide numbers are contextual references, not assertions of verbatim quotation. Scientific mechanisms added to elaborate the lectures (e.g., neonatal surfactant deficiency, modern neurophysiology nomenclature, Bohr effect and blood-brain barrier mechanics) are differentiated as contextual information in the articles or science-review notes.

Two important chemistry corrections preserve the exam association without propagating errors: most transported CO₂ uses the bicarbonate pathway, not carbonic acid as its stable transported form, and CO₂-rich blood is not actually blue. The lecture's older pontine center terms are explicitly marked as course terminology.
