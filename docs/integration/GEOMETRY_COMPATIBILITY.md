# Geometry compatibility and nerve comparison

The current displayed coordinate convention is meters, Y-up. That convention alone does **not** make two anatomies spatially interchangeable. Registration is model and source specific. The male BP3D converter records millimeters/Z-up → meters/Y-up and stage placement (`public/ATTRIBUTION.md`, `scripts/convert-anatomy.py`). HRA female input is already meters/Y-up and is stage translated (`scripts/convert-female.py`). The female study applies additional local affine fits and a body morph (`scripts/build-female-reconstruction.py`, fit report).

## Direct peripheral nerve comparison

MVMT's 16 parts represent 8 bilateral groups: brachial plexus, sciatic, femoral, common fibular, median, ulnar, radial and tibial. Their IDs are `NV-nerve-*-l/r`, their `source` is `schematic`, and the names in `atlas.json` are machine-style labels. PR #283 includes **all eight conceptually** among 27 bilateral named groups; its IDs are `ZN*`, and its tubes are derived from Z-Anatomy curves. The other 19 PR #283 groups are axillary, deep/superficial fibular, dorsal scapular, facial, lateral femoral cutaneous, lateral/medial plantar, long thoracic, mandibular, maxillary, musculocutaneous, obturator, ophthalmic, pudendal, saphenous, superior gluteal, suprascapular and trigeminal. Match this list by reviewed concept and side, not exact strings (`nerve-femoral` versus `Femoral nerve`). MVMT also has 120 **central** schematic structures that PR #283 does not address. BP3D's 139 existing nervous meshes remain a third representation class.

Source-derived curves have a stronger provenance path than author-defined schematic curves, but neither has demonstrated segment-by-segment anatomical fidelity against this model. PR #283's source export and per-bone residual report are absent from the tag; its current transform score is not a clinical accuracy measure. MVMT's fit confidence records envelope violations, not nerve course accuracy. Default choice for overlapping male peripheral concepts should wait for visual/path audit, side verification, duplicate checks against BP3D, license clearance and complete provenance. Female should receive no supplement until independent landmark fit, local deformation if needed, collision/containment checks, expert review and model-specific mapping pass.

## Migration implications

Do not upgrade BP3D now. A future version change requires source archive/hash/license capture, diff of FJ parts and FMA concepts, rederived transforms, regenerated chunks, crosswalk migration, all region/area memberships, search groups, nerve fits, and golden-image/geometry validation. A version string match alone is insufficient.
