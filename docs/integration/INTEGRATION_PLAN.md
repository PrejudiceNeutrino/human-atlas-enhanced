# Phased implementation plan

**Order:** model/identity/visibility contracts → region and teaching navigation → hide/restore → metadata and supplemental interface → nerve reconciliation → model-specific geometry → knowledge infrastructure. Each phase is a feature port into wiiiimm, not a donor merge. The two routed modes and all three bundled manifests remain regression inputs. Phase 0 ends with this audit; none of the following phases has begun.

Phase 1 is **ready for design/implementation**, with its scope limited to contracts and behavior-preserving adapters. Phases 5–7 are gated by missing source assets, scientific registration review and license clearance. The next implementation PR should include a baseline snapshot of male, HRA female and female study manifests before changing the loader. The current female-readiness gate reports integrity passed but readiness false because independent review and coverage are unresolved; Phase 1 must preserve that honest status, not manufacture a passing readiness result.

## Explicit decisions

- **Baseline:** wiiiimm is appropriate because it already supports male/HRA female/study modes and has r185 verification; its current monolithic state is a refactor target, not a reason to revert to upstream.
- **MVMT:** port regional/layer/bridge concepts selectively; reject exercise-program drills, Add buttons and parent-owned clinical authority. Its custom assets require separate review.
- **Regions and areas:** one canonical region system with areas as many-to-many teaching stations; PR #1 regex and centroid rules only seed candidate membership.
- **PR #421:** clean port with minor adaptation, not cherry-pick, due state and renderer divergence.
- **Nerves:** PR #283 source-derived tubes have broader named coverage; MVMT schematic paths may remain as labeled alternatives until evidence selects a default. No female use is currently established.
- **IDs:** internal immutable concept IDs plus typed FMA/UBERON/BP3D/HRA/Z-Anatomy/MVMT crosswalks; meshes remain model-specific representations.
- **Future content:** descriptions/Latin in cited knowledge records; pathology and medication links in separate datasets.

## Answers to the audit's 17 decision questions

1. **wiiiimm baseline?** Yes for application work: it preserves male/female support and current r185 behavior. Its reconstructed female is a study model, and HRA reference routing is unfinished.
2. **MVMT port/reject?** Port region navigation, depth filtering, reviewed bridge candidates and layer/line ideas. Reject exercise drills and parent-program authority. Defer geometry and clinical prose pending review.
3. **Can regions and areas coexist?** Yes, as separate many-to-many knowledge relations; keep only one region taxonomy.
4. **Hierarchy?** Body → reviewed region(s) → teaching area(s) → canonical concepts → model-specific mesh representations. Areas may span regions.
5. **PR #421?** Port with minor adaptation; its three-file patch cannot be applied directly over the changed wiiiimm state/renderer.
6. **PR #283 versus MVMT nerves?** 27 bilateral source-derived named groups versus eight bilateral schematic peripheral groups, with all eight conceptually overlapping; MVMT additionally has central schematics.
7. **Registered versus schematic?** MVMT Z-Anatomy fascia/ligaments/muscles are male-fitted; its nerves are authored schematic. PR #283 derives tubes from source curves then registers them to male bones. Registration does not itself prove fidelity.
8. **Male-only geometry?** All MVMT fitted/schematic supplements and PR #283 nerves are evidenced only in the male BP3D frame.
9. **Female supplement requirements?** Independent source-to-female transform, landmarks, local fit/collision and side checks, model crosswalk, expert review, license/provenance record.
10. **Canonical IDs?** Immutable internal concept IDs with typed FMA/UBERON/etc. mappings; no English-name key.
11. **Concept to meshes?** A reviewed many-to-many `MeshRepresentation.conceptLinks` relation, with part/whole semantics and side.
12. **Concept to models?** Resolve the same concept through per-model representation sets; zero meshes is a valid result.
13. **Descriptions and Latin?** Versioned cited knowledge records and terminology, outside geometry manifests.
14. **Pathology/medications?** Separate condition/drug collections with sourced relationship records to anatomy and each other.
15. **Licenses?** MIT code; BP3D/HRA CC BY obligations; Z-Anatomy derived assets invoke CC BY-SA and require object-level rights review. Preserve and extend credits.
16. **Highest risks?** Female spatial overlays, Z-Anatomy/MVMT chain of title, nerve duplication/fidelity, and renderer/loader divergence.
17. **Least-redo order?** Contracts and IDs, regions, areas, hide/restore, supplemental metadata, nerve decision, female registration, then long-term knowledge ingestion.
