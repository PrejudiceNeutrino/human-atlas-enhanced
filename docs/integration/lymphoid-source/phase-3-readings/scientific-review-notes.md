**Other necessary qualifications:**

- The three “lymphoid organs” answer is the lecturer's **lymph nodes, thymus, spleen** classification. Bone marrow also qualifies as primary lymphoid organ in broader immunology.
- A lymph-node flow schematic is not a literal sequence of fluid passing through B cells and then T cells. It moves through sinuses beside those cell-rich regions.
- Most nucleated cells normally express MHC I. Pathogen-associated peptide presentation and appropriately activated CD8-cell recognition are the relevant steps, not merely the presence of MHC I.
- MHC II presentation can activate helper T cells and promote appropriate B-cell responses; it does not automatically make antibodies without intervening events.
- Cytotoxic T and NK cell mechanisms involve **perforin/granzymes** and regulated cell-death pathways; calling them simply secreted “defensins” is not a reliable mechanistic equivalence.
- Thymic **positive and negative selection** and **affinity maturation** extend beyond the slide-level explanations. They are labeled scientific context in the extended “Who Cares?” pieces.
- All numeric thresholds and antibody timing illustrations are contextual rather than universal biological laws.
- HIV does not make the common cold automatically fatal; autoimmunity is not exclusively mediated by antibodies.
- The classical signs are **rubor, calor, tumor, dolor**. The lecturer's slide has the typo “dolar.”
- The lecture's “five tonsil” count is reproduced as course terminology, with an anatomical note about lingual tonsillar nodules.
